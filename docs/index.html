Simple by design

LOW handles old baseline caches and logs. MID adds known re-downloadable app caches. HIGH adds authorized developer caches with an extra confirmation. LEFTOVERS keeps every item unselected until you choose it and moves approved items to Trash.

Private by default

Everything happens locally. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or data upload. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and works only with the Home folder you approve.
