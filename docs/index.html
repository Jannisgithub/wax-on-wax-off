Choose one mode, run a read-only analysis, review the exact plan, then confirm what should be removed.

Simple by design

LOW handles old baseline caches and logs. MID adds known re-downloadable app caches, including closed-app Chrome and Spotify disk caches while preserving profiles and persistent storage. HIGH adds package caches and closed-app render caches; every HIGH-only action starts unchecked, and MID is recommended when the extra immediate cleanup is under 1 GB. LEFTOVERS keeps every item unselected until you choose it and moves approved items to Trash.

Measured, fixed locations

The app limits cleanup to documented cache locations and never scans project folders or protected personal folders such as Downloads, Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Movies, or Music. Cleanup history shows how much of a previous direct deletion remains reclaimed, using only local aggregate metadata.

Private by default

Everything happens locally. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or data upload. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and works only with the Home folder you approve.
