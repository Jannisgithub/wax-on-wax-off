FAQ

What does the app do?

Wax On/Wax Off reviews local cleanup items on your Mac, such as old caches, logs, crash reports, package caches, and Application Leftovers. You choose a mode, run analysis, review the plan, and confirm before anything changes.

Does the app delete files automatically?

No. Analysis is read-only. No files are removed without confirmation.

Why do I see a file name during analysis or cleanup?

Real cleanup shows one discreet current-processing row while it works. Paths are shortened to Home-relative form, such as ~/Library/Caches/..., and are not saved as a file log.

What is Demo Mode?

Demo Mode is a separate selectable sample workflow. It uses built-in sample data so you can choose and preview LOW, MID, HIGH, and APPLICATION LEFTOVERS workflows. Demo Mode does not scan, move, or delete real files, and Skip demo is always available during the demo.

What is LOW / MID / HIGH?

LOW reviews old caches and logs using a 30-day rule. MID adds known app caches using a 14-day rule. HIGH adds reviewed developer, package, and closed-app render caches; HIGH-only items start unchecked.

What are APPLICATION LEFTOVERS?

APPLICATION LEFTOVERS are possible inactive app data items grouped by bundle identifier with Home-relative evidence paths. They are never selected automatically, and selected items are moved to Trash.

How do I use real cleanup?

Open Wax On/Wax Off, click Open Full Version, choose a cleanup mode, select your Home folder, then review and confirm selected items before anything changes.

Why does the app ask for my Home folder?

The Mac App Store build runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It can only inspect cleanup candidates after you choose your own Home folder in the standard macOS folder picker. Do not enable Full Disk Access; the app does not need it. The app rejects system folders, /Users, other user folders, and lookalike folders.

Does the app scan Documents, Desktop, or Downloads?

No. Protected personal folders such as Documents, Desktop, Downloads, Pictures, Movies, and Music are not scanned. Cleanup is limited to approved local cleanup locations such as old caches, logs, crash reports, and reviewed package or developer caches.
